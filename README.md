🏥 Erinde – National Smart Health Access & Flow Infrastructure








📌 Project Description

Erinde is a national digital health infrastructure designed to optimize patient flow, reduce hospital waiting times, and improve healthcare system efficiency across Rwanda.

It works through USSD and mobile integration, allowing every citizen — including those without smartphones — to:

Book appointments

Make payments

Receive queue numbers

Access healthcare services efficiently

Erinde does not replace existing systems like E-Ubuzima, but strengthens and extends them by focusing on operational efficiency, inclusion, and national-level intelligence.

💡 The Problem Erinde Solves

Despite digital health transformation, key challenges remain:

Long waiting times in hospitals

Overcrowding at referral facilities

Underused rural health centers

Limited access for non-smartphone users

Lack of real-time national patient flow monitoring

Weak integration between payments, insurance, and facility operations

Patients wasting time; hospitals struggling with congestion

Ministry of Health lacking live operational visibility

Erinde directly addresses these system-level gaps.

💡 How Erinde Works
1️⃣ Citizen Access (USSD + Mobile)

Patients can:

Dial a USSD code on any phone

Book appointments

Pay via mobile money

Receive queue numbers and estimated waiting times

Be directed to less crowded hospitals

No smartphone required.

2️⃣ Hospital Dashboard

Health facilities receive:

Real-time patient queue updates

Appointment management tools

Payment confirmation tracking

Patient flow analytics and service demand monitoring

This improves scheduling, staffing, and operational efficiency.

3️⃣ National Ministry Dashboard

The Ministry of Health can view:

National congestion heatmaps

Patient flow by district

Revenue and payment tracking

Service demand trends

Early warning signals for unusual disease patterns

During outbreaks (e.g., Marburg virus), Erinde can detect abnormal case increases early.

🚀 Future Strategic Capabilities

AI-based predictive patient flow and demand forecasting

Integration with health insurance (CBHI validation & automation)

Integration with drug supply systems to prevent stockouts

Real-time outbreak alerts and national health risk monitoring

Nationwide rollout to all public and private health facilities

⭐ What Makes Erinde Unique

Inclusive by Design: Works on basic phones using USSD.

National Operational Focus: Optimizes hospital load across the entire country.

Predictive Intelligence: Uses AI to forecast congestion and health trends before escalation.

Financial Transparency Layer: Real-time mobile money integration and automated reconciliation.

Complementary Architecture: Strengthens existing systems rather than competing.

Infrastructure-Level Vision: Built as a long-term national backbone, not a short-term app.

💰 Sustainability & Investment Value

Erinde is sustainable through:

Government infrastructure contracts

Telecom USSD revenue sharing

Private hospital subscriptions

Transaction-based processing fees

International health and digital development funding

Healthcare is continuous — making Erinde a stable long-term infrastructure investment.

🏗 Vision

Erinde aims to become Rwanda’s National Health Flow and Intelligence Infrastructure, ensuring:

Efficient hospitals

Reduced patient waiting times

Equal access for all citizens

Data-driven decision-making

Stronger national health security
